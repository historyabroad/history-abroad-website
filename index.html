The mission

What Is
History Abroad?

History Abroad began with a simple observation: the world is full of stories that no one is documenting. Not the famous ones - the quiet ones. A catfish in the Seine. An empty street in a medieval village. A field where something important once happened.

We travel. We observe. We film. No narration, no agenda - just the world as it is, preserved before it changes.

Everything here is history - because everything happening right now will one day be the past. This portfolio of history has been compiled over many years, both present and past that will be published over the coming years. Subscribing and supporting our channel helps History Abroad exist.
